# Code of Conduct

AlgoSphere is a technical research project. Questions, criticism and disagreement are welcome when they help make the work clearer, safer or more reliable.

## How to take part

Please keep public conversations constructive:

- discuss the idea, evidence or implementation rather than attacking people
- distinguish measured results from assumptions and opinions
- challenge claims with reproducible evidence or a clear reason where possible
- accept that a hypothesis can fail and that negative results are part of research
- keep private account information, credentials and security-sensitive data out of public issues
- do not use the project for trading-signal promotion, guaranteed-return claims, referral spam or unrelated advertising

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, doxxing and repeated spam are not welcome.

## Reporting a problem

For normal project discussions, documentation problems or research disagreements, use the relevant GitHub issue form.

For security-sensitive information, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) and do not publish exploitable details in a public issue.

For harassment or abuse that should not be handled in a public thread, use GitHub's own reporting and moderation tools rather than posting personal or sensitive information publicly.

## Why this exists

The goal is simple: keep the public project useful for people interested in quantitative research, machine learning, market analysis and the engineering behind AlgoSphere.

Constructive disagreement is part of that. Personal attacks are not.
