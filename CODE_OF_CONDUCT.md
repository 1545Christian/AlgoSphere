# Code of Conduct

AlgoSphere is a technical research project. Questions, criticism and disagreement are welcome when they help make the work clearer or more reliable.

Please keep public conversations constructive:

- discuss the idea, evidence or implementation rather than attacking people
- distinguish measured results from assumptions and opinions
- do not post private account information, credentials or security-sensitive data
- do not use issues to promote trading signals, guaranteed returns, referral spam or unrelated services
- when challenging a result, include reproducible evidence or a clear reason where possible
- accept that a hypothesis can fail and that negative results are part of research

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, doxxing and repeated spam are not welcome.

The goal is simple: keep the public project useful for people interested in quantitative research, machine learning, market analysis and the engineering behind AlgoSphere.
