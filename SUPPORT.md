# Support and Questions

If you have a question about AlgoSphere, the fastest route depends on the topic.

## General questions

Check the [FAQ](docs/FAQ.md) first.

## Bug or documentation problem

Open a GitHub issue and include enough detail to reproduce or verify the problem.

## Research idea

Open a research-question issue and describe:

- the hypothesis
- why it may matter
- what evidence would support or reject it
- how it should be compared fairly with the current baseline

## Security issue

Please follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Do not publish credentials, private account data or security-sensitive information in a public issue.

## Trading / investment requests

AlgoSphere is not a signal service and this repository does not provide personal investment advice.
