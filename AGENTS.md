## Public disclosure boundary

Public documentation must remain reconstruction-resistant.

Do not publish or newly expose:
- private trading/application source
- credentials, tokens, account data or deployment secrets
- exact strategy names, parameters, thresholds or signal formulas
- exact feature/label sets, model hyperparameters, weights or scoring/ranking logic
- exact entry/exit prices, sizing, leverage, stop/target or risk rules
- private prompts/contracts or execution-routing logic
- private schema/table/column mappings or internal topology
- combinations of otherwise harmless details that materially reduce the effort required to reconstruct the private system

Prefer high-level, evidence-based descriptions of what changed, what is proven, what remains open and what is deliberately private.

## Historical integrity

Do not rewrite historical dated evidence simply to modernize the brand. When clarification is useful, state once that AlgoSphere is now MIOIQ while preserving the original historical context.

## Status discipline
